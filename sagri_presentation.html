Smart Crop Recommender

📥 Multi-Dimensional Inputs

🌡 Temperature (°C) 💧 Humidity (%) 🌧 Rainfall (mm) ⚗️ Soil pH 🧪 Nitrogen (N) 🧪 Phosphorus (P) 🧪 Potassium (K) 🏔 Elevation ☀️ Sunlight Hours 💨 VPD (kPa) 🏜 Sand % 🌊 Silt % 🧱 Clay % 📍 State/Region

⚙️ Algorithm Logic

1
Soil Texture Triangle Physics — compute drainage class and water-holding capacity from Sand/Silt/Clay proportions
2
Vapour Pressure Deficit formula: VPD = 0.6108 × e^(17.27T/T+237.3) × (1 - RH/100)
3
Random Forest classifier scores 22 crops; ICAR exclusion zones override unsuitable selections
4
Returns Top-5 ranked crops with per-crop suitability %, expected yield range, and market notes
