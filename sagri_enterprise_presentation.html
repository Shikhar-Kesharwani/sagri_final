Vapour Pressure Deficit Formulation
VPD = es(T) × (1 - RH / 100)
where es(T) = 0.6108 × exp[ (17.27 × T) / (T + 237.3) ]
VPD quantifies the atmospheric moisture extraction force exerted on crop foliage. SAGRI pairs VPD with hydraulic conductivity calculated from ternary Sand/Silt/Clay fractions.
