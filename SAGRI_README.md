# SAGRI - Krishi Shayak 🌾

## Smart Agriculture Platform for Indian Farmers

SAGRI (Smart Agriculture Real-time Intelligence) is an advanced AI-powered farming assistant designed specifically for Indian farmers. The platform combines modern technology with user-friendly design to help farmers make data-driven decisions and maximize their crop yields.

---

## 🚀 Features

### Core AI Features
- **🔬 Crop Disease Detection** - Upload crop images for instant AI-powered disease diagnosis
- **⚠️ Crop Failure Risk Prediction** - Predict risks based on soil, weather, and historical data
- **📈 Price Forecasting** - AI-powered crop price predictions with trend analysis
- **🌱 Smart Crop Recommendations** - Personalized crop suggestions based on your conditions

### Additional Features
- **📅 AI Crop Calendar** - Optimal timing for sowing, irrigation, and harvesting
- **🌤️ Weather Intelligence Dashboard** - Real-time weather insights and forecasts
- **💰 Market Price Comparison** - Compare prices across different mandis
- **👥 Farmer Community** - Connect and share knowledge with fellow farmers
- **💬 Expert Connect** - Chat with agricultural experts for professional advice
- **📋 Government Schemes** - Browse subsidies and benefits
- **🌍 Soil Health Reports** - Comprehensive soil analysis and recommendations
- **📊 Prediction History** - Track all your past predictions and analyses

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Landing Page** - Learn about SAGRI features
2. **Login** - Click "Get Started" or "Login" button
3. **Enter Phone** - Provide 10-digit mobile number
4. **Verify OTP** - Enter the OTP (any 6 digits for demo)
5. **Select Role** - Choose Farmer or Admin
6. **Enter Name** - Provide your name
7. **Dashboard** - Access all features!
